### Prompt final compartilhado — v2

Substitua `{id}` e `{scene}` pelos valores da linha correspondente acima.

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: portrait environment photograph for a Brazilian home-improvement ecommerce navigation card, category {id}.
Primary request: one realistic photograph of {scene}.
Housing context: a well-kept comfortable Brazilian middle-class apartment or small house, attainable contemporary renovation, ordinary room dimensions, practical materials and furnishings. Not impoverished, not luxurious, not a mansion or resort.
Composition/framing: single 3:4 vertical photo, eye-level with a slight downward angle showing a meaningful area of the floor and finishing materials in the central and lower-middle frame. Room identity must remain clear in a central landscape thumbnail crop. The finishes, floor colors and tile textures are important.
Lighting/mood: natural Brazilian daytime light, neutral white balance, believable colors, moderately soft shadows, realistic material texture, inviting lived-in feeling without clutter.
Styling: use the exact distinct finish colors in the scene description rather than applying the same beige-oak palette everywhere. Consistent photographic quality, but genuinely different homes and materials.
Constraints: no people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no border, no gradient or UI baked into the image, not a collage. Avoid enormous rooms, floor-to-ceiling panoramic glazing, expansive mountain views, luxury marble, elaborate designer furniture or dramatic hotel lighting.
```

## Prompt final compartilhado

Para reproduzir cada prompt final, substitua `{id}` e `{scene}` pelos valores da linha correspondente acima.

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: rectangular landscape thumbnail for the {id} environment in a home improvement website menu.
Primary request: one realistic architectural photograph of {scene}.
Composition/framing: a single complete room/space viewed at eye level in a wide 3:2 landscape composition. Main identifying features must be legible at small thumbnail sizes. Not a collage, not an isolated product icon.
Lighting/mood: soft natural daytime light, balanced exposure, gentle shadows, calm welcoming atmosphere.
Color palette: consistent muted warm neutrals across this collection: ivory, sand, greige, pale natural oak and restrained charcoal accents. Outdoors keep foliage and blue water softly desaturated.
Materials/textures: plausible stone, ceramic, natural wood and woven fabrics, realistic but uncluttered.
Constraints: no people, no text, no lettering, no brand logos, no watermark, no border, no UI. One photo only.
```
